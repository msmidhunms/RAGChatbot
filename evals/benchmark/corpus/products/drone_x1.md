# Drone X1

The Drone X1 is a quadcopter for crop monitoring and infrastructure inspection.

## Specifications

- Flight time: 38 minutes per battery
- Maximum payload: 1.2 kg
- Weight without payload: 2.9 kg
- Control range: 8 km
- Ingress protection: IP54
- Camera: 48 megapixel with 3-axis gimbal

## Operation

The Drone X1 returns home automatically when the battery drops below 15 percent.
It can operate in temperatures from -10 to 40 degrees Celsius.
