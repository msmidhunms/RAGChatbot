# Rover R2

The Rover R2 is a four-wheeled ground robot for warehouse and field inspection.

## Specifications

- Battery life: 11 hours of continuous operation
- Top speed: 12 km/h
- Payload capacity: 40 kg
- Ingress protection: IP67
- Navigation: lidar and stereo cameras

## Operation

The Rover R2 docks itself for charging; a full charge takes 3 hours.
An optional lidar mast accessory raises the sensor to 1.5 metres for better range.
