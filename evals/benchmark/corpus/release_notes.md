# Release Notes

## Firmware 4.2

Released on 12 March 2024.
Adds obstacle avoidance v3, which detects thin wires and branches down to 8 mm in diameter.
Improves GPS lock time in cold weather by about 40 percent.

## Firmware 4.1

Released on 9 November 2023.
Fixes a battery calibration bug that reported up to 6 percent too much remaining charge.
Adds support for the Rover R2 lidar mast accessory.

## Fleet Cloud 2.8

Released on 2 April 2024.
Fleet maps can now be exported as GeoJSON files.
Adds role-based access control with three roles: viewer, operator and administrator.
