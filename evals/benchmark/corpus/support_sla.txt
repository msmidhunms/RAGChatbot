Nimbus Robotics Support Service Level Agreement

Support plans

Standard support: first response within one business day, available Monday to Friday, 08:00-18:00 CET.
Priority support: first response within four hours during business days.
Enterprise support: first response within one hour, available 24/7 including weekends and holidays.

Fleet Cloud availability

The Fleet Cloud portal has a monthly uptime commitment of 99.9 percent.
For every 0.1 percent below the commitment, customers receive a service credit of 10 percent of the monthly fee.
Planned maintenance is announced at least 72 hours in advance and does not count as downtime.

Escalation

Enterprise customers have a named technical account manager.
Unresolved Priority tickets are escalated to an engineering lead after 24 hours.
