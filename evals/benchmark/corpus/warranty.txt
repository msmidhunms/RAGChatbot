Nimbus Robotics Hardware Warranty

All Nimbus hardware comes with a 24-month limited warranty from the date of delivery.
Batteries are covered for 12 months or 300 charge cycles, whichever comes first.
Water damage is only covered when the device was used within its rated IP protection level.
Crash damage caused by pilot error is not covered.

Extended warranty

The Nimbus Care extended warranty adds one more year, for three years of coverage in total.
Nimbus Care costs 390 EUR per device and must be purchased within 60 days of delivery.
Nimbus Care includes one accidental damage replacement.
