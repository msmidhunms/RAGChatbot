# Information Security Policy

## Accounts and Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and may not be reused across systems.
Multi-factor authentication with a hardware security key is mandatory for all company accounts.
Access rights are reviewed every quarter by each team lead.

## Devices

All laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity.
Personal USB storage devices are not allowed on engineering workstations.

## Incidents

Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@nimbus.example within two hours of discovery.
The security team runs a tabletop incident exercise twice a year.

## Data Retention

Customer telemetry data is retained for 18 months and then deleted automatically.
Flight logs needed for warranty claims are kept for the duration of the warranty.
