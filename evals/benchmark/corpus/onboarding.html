New Hire Onboarding

Welcome to Nimbus Robotics! This page explains your first week.

Day One

Pick up your laptop at the reception desk at 09:00 and meet your onboarding buddy.

Your buddy is a colleague from another team who answers practical questions during your first month.

Safety Training

The Robotics Lab Safety 101 course is mandatory before you can enter the test lab.

The course must be completed within your first five working days.

Tools

  • Code lives in the internal Git server called Forge.
  • Team chat runs on the Beacon messaging app.
