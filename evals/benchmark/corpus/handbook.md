# Nimbus Robotics Employee Handbook

Nimbus Robotics builds autonomous drones and ground rovers for agricultural and industrial inspection.
The company was founded in 2019 in Larkspur by Ilse Varga, who is still the chief executive officer.
Nimbus Robotics has about 140 employees across engineering, operations and customer success.

## Working Hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time; outside core hours schedules are flexible.
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing their probation period.
The probation period lasts six months.

## Time Off

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid vacation per calendar year.
Up to five unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year.
Parental leave is 20 weeks at full pay for every parent.

## Expenses

Expenses above 500 EUR need written approval from your manager before purchase.
Receipts must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days.
Travel is booked through the travel desk; economy class is the default for flights under six hours.
